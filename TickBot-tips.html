Fast Answers (FAQ)

My Chromebook is frozen 😵
Hold the power button for ~10 seconds to force it off. Then turn it back on.
I can’t log in 🔐
Check Caps Lock. Type slowly. If it still won’t work, use “Forgot password” or make a ticket.
Wi-Fi won’t connect 📶
Turn Wi-Fi off and on. Reconnect to the school Wi-Fi. If it keeps failing, make a ticket.
Printing won’t print 🖨️
Students do not have access to school printers, if you need something printed please ask youtr teacher or make a LOW priority ticket
