Tell a Teacher 🗣️

This goes to your teacher to read. Be kind and honest.

Quick rule: If someone is hurt or unsafe, tell a grown-up right now.

1) What is this about?

2) Where did it happen?

If you’re not sure, that’s okay.

3) Who is this about?

Only share names if you know them. No last names needed.

4) What happened? (1–2 sentences)

Keep it short. Your teacher will help.

5) Is anyone hurt or unsafe?

If you picked 🚨 Yes, tell a grown-up right now too.
